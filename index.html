About

I am an undergraduate student in Automation at Northeastern University, China. My research interests include large language models, embodied AI, and time-series forecasting. My current work focuses on multivariate forecasting, in particular cross-channel information modeling and the reliability of exogenous variables, with a broader interest in building intelligent systems.

Research Interests

Large Language Models

Understanding large language models and applying them as components of intelligent systems.

Embodied AI

Intelligent agents that perceive, reason, and act in physical environments.

Time-Series Forecasting

Current research area, including:

  • Multivariate time-series forecasting

  • Foundation models / Transformer-based forecasting
