About

I'm an undergraduate in the Automation program at Northeastern University, China. My research so far is in multivariate time-series forecasting: how channels should exchange information, and when the exogenous variables a model relies on can be trusted.

That work has led to a journal paper, a conference paper, and two patent applications. Beyond forecasting, I'm interested in large language models and embodied AI.
